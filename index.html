Carbon Offsets You Can Actually Trust

The Challenge: The voluntary carbon market is plagued by fraud and double-counting. Companies pay for offsets that may not represent real, additional, or permanent carbon sequestration — exposing them to reputational and regulatory risk.

The EcoCredit Solution: Our AI continuously analyzes satellite imagery, LiDAR data, and ground sensors to verify that forests actually exist, are growing healthily, and are sequestering the claimed amount of CO₂. Every verified credit is tokenized on the blockchain — ensuring transparency, preventing double-counting, and enabling instant retirement.
